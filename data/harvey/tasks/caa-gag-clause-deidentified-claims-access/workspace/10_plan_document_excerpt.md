# HALVORSEN INDUSTRIAL GROUP EMPLOYEE HEALTH PLAN
## Plan Document (excerpt) - Amended and Restated effective January 1, 2024

**1.3 Plan Year.** The Plan Year is the calendar year.

**1.5 Funding.** The Plan is self-funded. Benefits are paid from the general assets of the
Company and from Participant contributions. The Company has purchased specific stop-loss
coverage with an attachment point of $750,000 per covered individual.

**2.1 Eligibility.** Regular full-time employees of the Company and its participating
subsidiaries scheduled to work 30 or more hours per week, and their eligible dependents. As of
January 1, 2026 the Plan covered 6,214 employees and 14,790 total covered individuals.

**8.1 Plan Sponsor.** Halvorsen Industrial Group, Inc. (EIN 36-XXXXXXX) is the Plan Sponsor.
The Plan is Plan Number 501.

**8.2 Plan Administrator and Named Fiduciary.** The Benefits Committee appointed by the
Company's Board of Directors is the Plan Administrator and named fiduciary within the meaning
of ERISA § 402(a). The Committee has discretionary authority to interpret the Plan and to
select, monitor and replace service providers, and may delegate ministerial claims
administration to a third-party administrator.

**8.4 Claims Administrator.** The Committee has engaged Meridian Health Administrators, LLC
as Claims Administrator under an administrative services agreement. The Claims Administrator
is not a fiduciary of the Plan except to the extent it exercises discretionary authority in
deciding claims and appeals under Section 9.

**8.6 Plan Records.** The Committee shall maintain, or cause to be maintained, records
sufficient to determine the benefits due to Participants and to demonstrate the Plan's
compliance with applicable law, and may require the Claims Administrator to furnish such
records.
