# MERIDIAN HEALTH ADMINISTRATORS, LLC
# THIRD-PARTY DATA USE AGREEMENT (Form TPDUA-2023.2)

This Third-Party Data Use Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by Meridian Health
Administrators, LLC ("Meridian") and the undersigned recipient ("Recipient") in connection
with Recipient's engagement by a Meridian client plan sponsor ("Client").

1. **Approved Purpose.** Meridian will make available to Recipient the data described in
Schedule 1 ("Data") solely for the purpose described in Schedule 1 as approved by Meridian in
writing (the "Approved Purpose"). Recipient shall not use the Data for any other purpose,
including without limitation the solicitation of Client or any other Meridian client, the
development of competing products or networks, or any benchmarking or repricing analysis not
expressly included in the Approved Purpose.

2. **Representations.** Recipient represents that it is not a Competitor (as defined in the
Client's administrative services agreement with Meridian) and will notify Meridian within five
business days if it becomes one.

3. **Confidentiality.** Recipient acknowledges that the Data contains Meridian Proprietary
Information, including provider reimbursement rates and Allowed Amounts, and shall hold the
Data in strict confidence, shall not disclose the Data or any analysis revealing provider-level
rates to Client's other vendors or to any third party, and shall not disclose the Data to Client
except in aggregated form approved by Meridian.

4. **Security.** Recipient shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards
no less protective than those required of business associates under 45 C.F.R. Part 164,
Subpart C, and shall access the Data only through Meridian's secure portal or an SFTP channel
designated by Meridian.

5. **Return and Destruction.** Within thirty days after completion of the Approved Purpose,
Recipient shall destroy all copies of the Data and certify destruction in writing.

6. **Audit.** Meridian may audit Recipient's compliance with this Agreement on ten business
days' notice.

7. **Term.** This Agreement terminates on completion of the Approved Purpose or twelve months
after the Effective Date, whichever is earlier.

8. **Governing Law; Injunctive Relief.** Delaware law. Recipient agrees that breach would cause
Meridian irreparable harm and consents to injunctive relief without bond.
