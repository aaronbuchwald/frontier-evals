# HALVORSEN INDUSTRIAL GROUP EMPLOYEE HEALTH PLAN
## Benefits Committee - Minutes of Meeting
### September 30, 2026 (videoconference)

**Present:** R. Halvorsen (CFO, Chair), D. Okafor (General Counsel), P. Raman (Director,
Benefits), L. Mendes (VP Human Resources), J. Park (Controller). **Also present:** A. Brandt
(Northgate Benefits Advisory), for item 3 only.

**1. Call to order.** The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:02 a.m. and confirmed a
quorum. The Committee acts as the Plan's named fiduciary and plan administrator under
Section 8.2 of the Plan document.

**2. Approval of minutes.** Minutes of the June 24, 2026 meeting were approved.

**3. Vendor monitoring - Meridian.** Ms. Brandt presented Northgate's proposed scope: (a)
benchmark Meridian's network discounts and administrative fees against regional reference
data; (b) a 2028 market check; (c) a 100% claims audit of the 2025 Plan Year by Claimsight
Audit Partners. Ms. Brandt noted that Meridian's Standard Reports do not contain claim-level
allowed amounts or provider identifiers and that benchmarking cannot be performed without
them. Ms. Raman summarized Meridian's September 18 response (custom extract fee of roughly
$140,000-$160,000, 180-day delivery, refusal of consent for Northgate as a "Competitor",
250-claim audit cap). Mr. Park observed that the Plan paid Meridian approximately $2.1 million
in administrative fees in 2025 and approximately $71 million in medical claims, and that the
Committee has never independently verified Meridian's network pricing. Mr. Halvorsen noted the
Committee's discussion at the June meeting of recent fiduciary litigation concerning employers'
access to claims data and the Committee's duty to monitor service providers, and said he was
"not comfortable signing another attestation we can't stand behind." Ms. Okafor advised that
she would engage outside counsel to review the ASA and the attestation question and report at
the October 28 meeting. Ms. Brandt left the meeting at 10:48 a.m.

**4. Gag clause attestation.** Ms. Raman reported that the 2026 attestation is due
December 31, 2026 and that she had submitted the 2024 and 2025 attestations herself through
the CMS HIOS portal after Meridian declined to submit on the Plan's behalf. She stated that
both prior attestations were submitted as "in compliance" without any additional information,
based on Meridian's 2024 written confirmation that its agreements do not contain gag clauses.

**5. Other business.** Mr. Mendes noted that open enrollment materials are due to print
October 20. No action required of the Committee.

**6. Next meeting.** October 28, 2026, 10:00 a.m. Agenda to include outside counsel's
report on the Meridian ASA.

The meeting adjourned at 11:05 a.m.
