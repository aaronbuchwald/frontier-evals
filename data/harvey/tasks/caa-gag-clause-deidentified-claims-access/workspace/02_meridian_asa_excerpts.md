## ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Allowed Amount**" means the amount payable for a Covered Service under the applicable
Network Provider contract or, for non-Network Providers, as determined under the Plan.

1.2 "**Claims Data**" means claim and encounter data processed by Meridian in the
administration of the Plan, in any form, including adjudication records, Allowed Amounts,
provider identifiers, procedure and diagnosis codes, and Participant eligibility data.

1.3 "**Competitor**" means any person that offers, directly or through an affiliate,
third-party administration, provider network, pharmacy benefit management, claims auditing,
or employee benefits consulting services in the United States.

1.4 "**Meridian Proprietary Information**" means Meridian's provider reimbursement rates,
fee schedules, Allowed Amounts, Network Provider contracts, pricing methodologies, claims
editing logic, and Standard Report formats, together with any compilation of Claims Data that
would reveal any of the foregoing.

1.5 "**Network**" means the Meridian Choice provider network made available to the Plan under
Article 3.

1.6 "**Participant**" has the meaning given in the Plan document and includes covered
dependents.

1.7 "**Standard Reports**" means the periodic reports described in Exhibit D, delivered through
the Meridian Insight portal.

## ARTICLE 9 - DATA AND REPORTS

**9.1 Ownership.** As between the parties, Claims Data is the property of the Plan. Meridian
Proprietary Information is and remains the property of Meridian, notwithstanding that it may be
embedded in or derivable from Claims Data.

**9.2 Standard Reports.** Meridian will deliver the Standard Reports monthly through the
Meridian Insight portal. Standard Reports present Claims Data in aggregated form by category of
service, provider type, and geography, and do not include claim-level Allowed Amounts or
provider-identifying fields.

**9.3 Claims Data Access.** Upon Plan Sponsor's written request, Meridian will make
de-identified Claims Data available to Plan Sponsor electronically through the Meridian Insight
portal in Meridian's then-standard extract format. Custom data extracts, including any extract
containing claim-level Allowed Amounts, Network Provider names or identifiers, or other fields
not included in the then-standard extract format, are available at Meridian's then-current
professional services rates (as of the Effective Date, $18,500 per extract plus $0.11 per claim
line) and will be delivered within one hundred eighty (180) days following Meridian's written
acceptance of the request. Meridian may decline any request for a custom extract that Meridian
reasonably determines would require disclosure of Meridian Proprietary Information or would
compromise the security or integrity of Meridian's systems.

**9.4 Third-Party Recipients.** Plan Sponsor shall not disclose, and shall not direct Meridian
to disclose, Claims Data or Standard Reports to any third party, including any consultant,
actuary, auditor, broker, or successor administrator, without Meridian's prior written consent.
Meridian will not unreasonably withhold consent with respect to a proposed recipient that
(i) executes Meridian's then-current Third-Party Data Use Agreement, (ii) is not a Competitor,
and (iii) agrees to use the Claims Data solely for the specific purpose approved in writing by
Meridian.

**9.5 Provider Rate Confidentiality.** Plan Sponsor acknowledges that Meridian's negotiated
reimbursement rates and Allowed Amounts are Meridian Proprietary Information. Plan Sponsor shall
not disclose provider-specific reimbursement rates, Allowed Amounts, or provider quality
ratings to Participants, Network Providers, referring providers, or the public. Meridian will
make cost estimates available to Participants through the Meridian member portal to the extent
required by applicable transparency laws.

**9.6 Claims Audits.** Plan Sponsor may, at its expense and not more than once per Plan Year,
cause an audit of claims paid by Meridian. Any audit shall (a) be limited to a statistically
valid random sample of not more than two hundred fifty (250) claims selected using Meridian's
sampling methodology, (b) be conducted by an auditor that is not a Competitor and has executed
Meridian's Third-Party Data Use Agreement, and (c) be performed on Meridian's premises or
through Meridian's secure view-only audit portal. Meridian will provide reasonable cooperation
with any audit meeting these requirements.

**9.7 Compliance with Law.** Nothing in this Article 9 shall be construed to restrict Plan
Sponsor's access to information in a manner prohibited by applicable law, including Section 724
of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974, as amended. To the extent any provision
of this Article 9 is determined by a court or governmental authority of competent jurisdiction
to be prohibited by applicable law, such provision shall be deemed modified to the minimum
extent necessary to comply.

**9.8 Survival.** Sections 9.1, 9.4 and 9.5 survive termination or expiration of this
Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

## ARTICLE 12 - TERM, TERMINATION AND AMENDMENT

**12.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues through
December 31, 2026 (the "Initial Term"). Thereafter this Agreement renews automatically for
successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least
ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

**12.2 Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement upon sixty (60) days'
written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within
such period.

**12.3 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both
parties.

**12.4 Changes in Law.** If a change in applicable law enacted after the Effective Date
requires modification of this Agreement or of the services provided hereunder, the parties shall
negotiate in good faith an amendment to implement such change, and Meridian may charge its
reasonable costs of implementing such change in accordance with Exhibit C.

**12.5 Transition Assistance.** Upon expiration or termination, Meridian will provide
transition assistance for up to one hundred eighty (180) days at Meridian's then-current
professional services rates, including delivery of Claims Data to Plan Sponsor or a successor
administrator in Meridian's then-standard extract format, subject to Sections 9.4 and 9.5.
