**1. Scope of Services.**

1.1 *Benchmarking.* Northgate will obtain claim-level medical claims data for the Plan from
the Plan's claims administrator and will benchmark the administrator's achieved network
discounts, allowed amounts by service category and geography, and administrative fees against
Northgate's regional reference database and publicly available Transparency in Coverage
machine-readable files. Deliverable: Benchmarking Report to the Committee, targeted for the
Committee's January 2027 meeting.

1.2 *Market Check.* Northgate will design and conduct a market check of third-party
administration and provider network services for the 2028 Plan Year, including preparation of
a request for proposal, evaluation of responses, and a recommendation to the Committee.
Deliverable: Market Check Report, targeted for June 2027.

1.3 *Claims Audit Coordination.* Northgate will coordinate the Committee's independent claims
audit, to be performed by Claimsight Audit Partners LLC under a separate engagement letter with
the Committee, including provision of the claims data set obtained under Section 1.1 to
Claimsight.

**2. Fees.**

2.1 *Fixed Fee.* $85,000 for the services in Sections 1.1 through 1.3, payable by Halvorsen
Industrial Group, Inc. (the "Company") in four equal installments on execution and on delivery
of each Deliverable.

2.2 *Transition Fee.* If, within eighteen (18) months after delivery of the Market Check
Report, the Plan enters into an agreement with a third-party administrator or network vendor
other than its incumbent administrator, the Company shall pay Northgate a transition fee of
$150,000, in consideration of Northgate's implementation support for the transition. No
transition fee is payable if the Plan renews with its incumbent administrator.

2.3 *Expenses.* Reasonable out-of-pocket expenses at cost, not to exceed $7,500 without the
Committee's approval.

**3. Northgate Marketplace.**

Northgate operates the Northgate Marketplace, a program through which third-party
administrators, network vendors, pharmacy benefit managers and point-solution vendors pay
Northgate an annual participation fee for inclusion in Northgate's vendor database and RFP
distribution lists. Participation fees are not contingent on any Northgate client's selection
of a participating vendor. Vendors responding to the Market Check may include Marketplace
participants. Additional information about the Marketplace program, including a list of
participants, is available to the Committee on request.

**5. Status; No Fiduciary Capacity.** Northgate provides consulting services only. Northgate
does not exercise discretionary authority or control over the Plan or its assets and is not a
fiduciary of the Plan. All decisions concerning the selection or retention of Plan service
providers are made solely by the Committee.

**6. Confidentiality; Data.** Northgate will execute a business associate agreement with the
Plan before receiving any protected health information and will hold all Plan data and any
administrator pricing information in confidence in accordance with the CSA.

**7. Compensation Disclosure.** Northgate's compensation for the services under this SOW is
as stated in Section 2. Northgate's Marketplace participation fees are described in Section 3.
