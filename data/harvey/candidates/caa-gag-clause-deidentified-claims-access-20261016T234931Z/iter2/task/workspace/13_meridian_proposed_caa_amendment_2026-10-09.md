# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES AGREEMENT
## (CAA Data Access Amendment)

**Meridian Form ASA-AM-CAA-2025.1**

This Amendment No. 1 (this "Amendment") to the Administrative Services Agreement effective
January 1, 2024 (the "Agreement") is entered into by **HALVORSEN INDUSTRIAL GROUP, INC.**, on
behalf of the Halvorsen Industrial Group Employee Health Plan ("Plan Sponsor" and the "Plan"),
and **MERIDIAN HEALTH ADMINISTRATORS, LLC** ("Meridian"). Capitalized terms not defined in this
Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

**Recitals.** Plan Sponsor has requested, and Meridian has agreed, to confirm and clarify the
parties' arrangements for Plan Sponsor's electronic access to de-identified Claims Data in
light of Section 724 of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 ("ERISA
Section 724"), on the terms set out below.

### 1. Additional Definitions

1.1 "**Data Access Request**" means a written request by Plan Sponsor for a Standard CAA
Extract, in the form of Schedule A-4, specifying the Recipient(s), if any, and the purpose for
which the Standard CAA Extract will be used.

1.2 "**Meridian Provider Key**" means a persistent pseudonymous identifier assigned by
Meridian to each Network Provider and non-Network Provider appearing in Claims Data, which is
included in the Standard CAA Extract in place of the provider's name, National Provider
Identifier, tax identification number, and street address, in furtherance of the
de-identification of Claims Data and the protection of Meridian Proprietary Information.
Meridian maintains the crosswalk between Meridian Provider Keys and provider identities as
Meridian Proprietary Information.

1.3 "**Recipient**" means a consultant, actuary, auditor, or other business associate (as
defined in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103) of the Plan designated by Plan Sponsor in a Data Access
Request.

1.4 "**Standard CAA Extract**" means the de-identified claim-level extract of Claims Data
containing the data elements listed in Schedule A-1, in Meridian's standard pipe-delimited
layout.

### 2. Section 9.3 (Claims Data Access) - Replaced

Section 9.3 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

> **9.3 Claims Data Access.**
>
> (a) *Standard CAA Extract.* Upon receipt of a complete Data Access Request, Meridian will
> deliver the Standard CAA Extract electronically, by secure file transfer (SFTP), to Plan
> Sponsor and to each Recipient identified in the Data Access Request, within ninety (90)
> days after Meridian's receipt of the complete Data Access Request.
>
> (b) *Frequency and Period.* Plan Sponsor may submit not more than two (2) Data Access
> Requests in any Plan Year. Each Standard CAA Extract will cover claims with paid dates
> within the twenty-four (24) months immediately preceding the date of the Data Access
> Request.
>
> (c) *Fees.* No fee will be charged for the first Standard CAA Extract delivered under this
> Section 9.3, provided this Amendment is executed by Plan Sponsor on or before October 30,
> 2026. Each subsequent Standard CAA Extract will be provided for a flat fee of $7,500,
> subject to annual adjustment in accordance with Exhibit C.
>
> (d) *Custom Extracts.* Any extract containing data elements not included in Schedule A-1,
> including Network Provider names, National Provider Identifiers, or other
> provider-identifying fields, is a custom extract. Custom extracts are available at
> Meridian's then-current professional services rates and will be delivered within one
> hundred eighty (180) days following Meridian's written acceptance of the request. Meridian
> may decline any request for a custom extract that Meridian reasonably determines would
> require disclosure of Meridian Proprietary Information or would compromise the security or
> integrity of Meridian's systems.
>
> (e) *De-identification.* The Standard CAA Extract is de-identified in accordance with
> 45 C.F.R. § 164.514(b)(2). Meridian will document the de-identification method on request.

### 3. Section 9.4 (Third-Party Recipients) - Replaced

Section 9.4 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following:

> **9.4 Recipients.**
>
> (a) Plan Sponsor may direct Meridian to deliver the Standard CAA Extract to a Recipient by
> identifying the Recipient and the purpose of the engagement in a Data Access Request
> delivered to Meridian not less than thirty (30) days before the requested delivery date.
> Each Recipient shall, before delivery, execute Meridian's Third-Party Data Use Agreement
> (Form TPDUA-2023.2) as modified by Schedule A-2.
>
> (b) If, within thirty (30) days after receipt of a Data Access Request, Meridian notifies
> Plan Sponsor in writing that Meridian reasonably believes the Recipient is a Competitor
> that intends to use the Standard CAA Extract to develop, market, or price a competing
> provider network or repricing product, the parties shall confer in good faith regarding
> appropriate additional protections for Meridian Proprietary Information. Meridian shall
> not be required to deliver the Standard CAA Extract to such Recipient until the parties
> have resolved Meridian's objection.
>
> (c) Plan Sponsor shall not otherwise disclose Claims Data or Standard Reports to any third
> party, other than a Recipient or as required by applicable law.

### 4. Section 9.5 (Provider Rate Confidentiality) - Amended

The second sentence of Section 9.5 is amended by adding at the end: ", except as required by
applicable law, including ERISA Section 724".

### 5. Section 9.6 (Claims Audits) - Amended

Section 9.6(a) is amended by replacing "two hundred fifty (250)" with "five hundred (500)".
Section 9.6(c) is amended by adding at the end: "or, at Meridian's election, through remote
access to such portal".

### 6. Acknowledgments

6.1 Plan Sponsor acknowledges and agrees that the Standard CAA Extract constitutes electronic
access to de-identified claims and encounter information on a per claim basis for purposes of
ERISA Section 724(a)(1)(B), and that the Agreement, as amended by this Amendment, contains no
provision prohibited by ERISA Section 724.

6.2 Plan Sponsor acknowledges that the Plan is solely responsible for the attestation
required by ERISA Section 724(a)(3) and that Meridian does not submit such attestation on
behalf of self-funded clients. Meridian makes no representation regarding the contents of
any agreement between Continental Mutual Health Insurance Company and any Network Provider.

6.3 Upon Plan Sponsor's request, Meridian will furnish a letter in Meridian's standard form
confirming the matters set out in Section 6.1 for Plan Sponsor's use in connection with its
attestation.

### 7. Release

In consideration of the fee waiver in Section 9.3(c) of the Agreement as amended, Plan
Sponsor, on behalf of itself, the Plan, and their respective fiduciaries, successors and
assigns, releases and discharges Meridian and its affiliates from any and all claims,
demands, losses and liabilities of any kind, known or unknown, arising out of or relating to
Plan Sponsor's or the Plan's access to Claims Data, Article 9 of the Agreement, or any
attestation submitted by or on behalf of the Plan under ERISA Section 724(a)(3) or the
corresponding provisions of the Public Health Service Act or the Internal Revenue Code, in
each case with respect to any period before the Amendment Effective Date, including any
claim for indemnification under Article 7 of the Agreement.

### 8. Effectiveness

8.1 This Amendment is effective on the later of (a) the date it has been executed by both
parties and (b) the date Meridian receives the written consent of Continental Mutual Health
Insurance Company under Section 4.3 of the Meridian Choice Network Access Agreement (the
"Amendment Effective Date"). Meridian will request Continental's consent promptly after
execution. If Continental's consent has not been received within one hundred twenty (120)
days after execution, either party may withdraw from this Amendment by written notice, in
which case the Agreement continues unamended.

8.2 The fee waiver in Section 9.3(c) of the Agreement as amended applies only if this
Amendment has been executed by Plan Sponsor on or before October 30, 2026.

### 9. No Other Changes

Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement (including Sections 9.1, 9.7,
9.8, 12.4 and 12.5 and Exhibit F) remains in full force and effect. In the event of a conflict
between this Amendment and the Agreement, this Amendment controls.
