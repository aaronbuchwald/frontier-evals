## A. Meridian email, November 2024

From: Meridian Client Compliance <compliance@meridianhealthadmin.com>
To: Priya Raman <praman@halvorsen-ind.com>
Date: November 6, 2024
Subject: CAA Gag Clause Prohibition Compliance Attestation - 2024

Dear Client,

As you may know, group health plans must submit a Gag Clause Prohibition Compliance
Attestation ("GCPCA") to CMS by December 31 of each year. **Meridian's policy is not to
submit the GCPCA on behalf of self-funded clients.** Plan sponsors should submit their own
attestation through the CMS HIOS GCPCA portal.

To assist you, Meridian confirms that **the Meridian administrative services agreement and
Meridian's provider network agreements do not contain gag clauses** within the meaning of the
Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2021. Meridian makes de-identified claims data available to
clients electronically through the Meridian Insight portal, and nothing in our agreements
restricts a client's access in a manner prohibited by law (see Section 9.7 of your ASA).

You may rely on this confirmation in completing your attestation.

Meridian Client Compliance Team
