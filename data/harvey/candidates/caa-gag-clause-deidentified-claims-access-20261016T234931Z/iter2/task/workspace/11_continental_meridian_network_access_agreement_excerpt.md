## ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Client Plan**" means a self-funded group health plan for which Meridian provides
administrative services and to which Meridian makes the Network available under this
Agreement.

1.2 "**Continental Rates**" means the reimbursement rates, fee schedules, per diems,
case rates, percent-of-charge arrangements and other payment terms contained in Continental's
agreements with Network Providers, and the allowed amounts resulting from the application of
such terms to individual claims.

1.3 "**Network**" means the Meridian Choice provider network, consisting of the Continental
provider agreements designated by Continental from time to time as available to Client Plans.

1.4 "**Network Provider**" means a provider party to a Continental provider agreement
included in the Network.

---

## ARTICLE 2 - NETWORK ACCESS

**2.1 License.** Continental grants Meridian a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to
make the Network available to Client Plans and to reprice Client Plan claims using Continental
Rates during the term of this Agreement.

**2.2 Network Provider Agreements.** Continental is and remains the sole contracting party
under the Continental provider agreements. Meridian shall not communicate with Network
Providers concerning Continental Rates except through the claims adjudication and provider
service processes described in Schedule 2.

---

## ARTICLE 4 - CONFIDENTIALITY OF CONTINENTAL RATES

**4.1 Confidential Information.** Continental Rates are Continental's confidential and
proprietary information. Meridian shall protect Continental Rates with at least the degree of
care it uses to protect its own confidential information of like kind.

**4.2 Disclosure to Client Plans.** Meridian may disclose Continental Rates to a Client Plan
(a) in aggregated form through Meridian's standard client reporting, and (b) on a claim-level
basis to the extent required by applicable law, provided in the case of clause (b) that
Meridian's administrative services agreement with the Client Plan contains confidentiality and
use restrictions with respect to Continental Rates no less protective of Continental than
Article 9 of Meridian's standard form administrative services agreement as approved by
Continental.

**4.3 Disclosure to Client Plan Third Parties.** Meridian shall not disclose Continental Rates
or claim-level allowed amounts to, and shall not permit any Client Plan to disclose
Continental Rates or claim-level allowed amounts to, any consultant, broker, actuary, auditor,
repricing vendor, or competing administrator or network of a Client Plan without Continental's
prior written consent, which Continental may grant or withhold in its sole discretion.
Meridian shall include in each Client Plan administrative services agreement provisions
sufficient to give effect to this Section 4.3.

**4.4 Participant Cost Information.** Nothing in this Article 4 restricts Meridian or a
Client Plan from making available to a Client Plan's participants and beneficiaries the
cost-sharing and provider-specific cost information required to be made available under the
federal transparency-in-coverage regulations and Division BB of the Consolidated
Appropriations Act, 2021.

**4.5 Provider Agreements.** Continental represents that, as of the Effective Date, its
Network Provider agreements do not prohibit Continental or its licensees from disclosing
provider-specific cost or quality information to Client Plans or their participants to the
extent required by applicable federal law.

---

## ARTICLE 9 - GENERAL

**9.3 Compliance.** Each party shall comply with applicable law in performing this Agreement.
The parties shall cooperate in good faith to implement any modification to this Agreement
that is required by a change in applicable law, including any regulations or guidance issued
under Division BB of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2021.

**9.6 Third-Party Beneficiaries.** This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties.
No Client Plan or other person is a third-party beneficiary of this Agreement.
