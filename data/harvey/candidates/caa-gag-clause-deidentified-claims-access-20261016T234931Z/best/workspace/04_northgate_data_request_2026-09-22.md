Re: Claims data request - Halvorsen Industrial Group Employee Health Plan (the "Plan")

Dear Priya,

As engaged by the Plan's Benefits Committee under our Statement of Work dated August 20, 2026,
Northgate requests the following de-identified claims and encounter data from Meridian Health
Administrators for the period October 1, 2023 through September 30, 2026 (36 months) for
medical claims administered under the Plan:

**Claim-level fields requested (per claim line):**
- De-identified member key (consistent across claims; no name, SSN, address, DOB, or member ID)
- Member relationship code (employee / spouse / dependent) and age band
- Claim number, line number, dates of service, claim received and paid dates
- Place of service, claim type (facility / professional), inpatient/outpatient indicator
- Rendering and billing provider NPI, provider name, provider specialty / clinical designation
- Network indicator (in-network / out-of-network) and network tier
- Procedure code (CPT/HCPCS) with modifiers, revenue code, DRG, diagnosis codes
- Units, billed charges, **allowed amount**, plan paid amount, member deductible / copay /
  coinsurance, coordination of benefits amount
- Pricing method indicator (e.g., contracted fee schedule, percent of charges, per diem)

**Purposes.** (1) Network discount and allowed-amount benchmarking against regional
reference data for the Committee's annual review of Meridian's performance and fees;
(2) preparation of a 2028 TPA/network market check; (3) provision of the same data set to
Claimsight Audit Partners, the Committee's proposed independent claims auditor, for a
100%-population claims audit (not a sample audit) covering the 2025 Plan Year.

**Format and delivery.** Pipe-delimited flat files or an SFTP data feed; Northgate's data
dictionary is attached. We ask for delivery by November 15, 2026.

**Confidentiality.** Northgate and Claimsight will each execute a HIPAA business associate
agreement with the Plan and will hold any Meridian pricing information in confidence, use it
solely for the Plan's purposes, and not disclose it publicly or to other clients. We are
prepared to negotiate reasonable confidentiality terms with Meridian but cannot accept a
purpose restriction that prevents the Committee from using the analysis in a market check.

Please let us know if Meridian requires anything further.
