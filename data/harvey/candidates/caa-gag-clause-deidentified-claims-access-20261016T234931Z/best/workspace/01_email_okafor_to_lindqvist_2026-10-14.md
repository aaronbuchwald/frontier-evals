Background

Halvorsen Industrial Group sponsors a self-funded medical plan (about 6,200 employees, roughly
14,800 covered lives). Meridian Health Administrators has been our TPA since January 1, 2024
under a three-year administrative services agreement; before that we were with Cordell Benefit
Services for about nine years. Meridian administers claims and gives us access to the Meridian
Choice provider network. Meridian is a subsidiary of Continental Mutual Health, a national
carrier; the Meridian Choice network and the provider contracts behind it belong to
Continental, and Meridian uses them under an intercompany network access agreement. We have an
excerpt of that intercompany agreement in the 2023 RFP binder (Meridian gave it to our
then-consultant when we asked how network pricing would be reported); Priya is adding it to
the folder along with Northgate's statement of work.

In September our benefits consultant, Northgate Benefits Advisory, asked Meridian (through
Priya) for 36 months of de-identified claim-level data so that Northgate can (a) benchmark
Meridian's network discounts and administrative fees for the Committee, (b) run a market check
/ RFP for 2028, and (c) have an independent firm, Claimsight Audit Partners, audit paid claims.
The Committee asked for this after reading about the recent fiduciary litigation over
employers' access to their own claims data.

Meridian's position, in short: what we want is available through its portal in Meridian's
standard formats; anything with claim-level allowed amounts or provider identifiers is a
"custom extract" at $18,500 plus $0.11 per claim line with a 180-day delivery window; Northgate
is a "Competitor" under the ASA so Meridian will not consent to us sharing data with them; and
Claimsight can audit a sample of 250 claims once a year on Meridian's terms. Tom Delacroix at
Meridian also says the ASA cannot contain a gag clause "by definition" because of the
compliance-with-law language in Section 9.7, and that the CAA does not tell TPAs how, how fast,
or at what price to deliver data.

Priya submitted our gag clause attestations in December 2024 and December 2025 as unqualified
"in compliance" attestations. She did so in reliance on Meridian's written statement in 2024
that its agreements do not contain gag clauses. Meridian told us at the time it does not submit
attestations on behalf of self-funded clients.

What we need

A memo I can take to the Committee on October 28 that answers:

1. Does Article 9 of the ASA (or anything else in it) contain provisions prohibited by the
   CAA's gag clause rule? Which ones, and how confident are we? Where the law is unclear, say so.
2. What should we do about Northgate's request and the Claimsight audit? Can Meridian block
   Northgate because it is a "Competitor"?
3. What is our exposure on the two attestations already filed, and what do we do about the
   one due December 31, 2026 if Meridian has not fixed the contract by then?
4. What amendment should we ask Meridian for, and how should we approach the negotiation
   given where we are in the contract term?

Please also flag anything else the Committee, as plan fiduciary, should be thinking about -
including anything about how the data should move between Meridian, Northgate, Claimsight and
us, and anything about the Northgate engagement itself. Rolf (our CFO, who chairs the
Committee) has asked specifically what the realistic dollar exposure is if we have been out of
compliance since 2024; he does not want a number we cannot explain.

We have not yet raised any of this with Meridian's legal department; so far it has all been at
the account-management level.
